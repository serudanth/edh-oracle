# fowlplays — Simulated Player Persona
*Adaptive Combat-Engine Architect (The Host)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Adaptive Combat-Engine Architect
- **Threat Level:** ★★★☆☆ (Median Threat: 51.1 Typical, Power Score: 5.2 / 10.0)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Johnny / Spike-curious**. Builds wide across 13+ distinct archetypes without cloning shells. Values mechanical discovery, commander-specific engines, and intricate synergies over generic goodstuff.
- **Ceiling / Floor:** Sits comfortably at Bracket 2–3 casual-midrange, but conceals an asymmetric ceiling:
  - *The Rush* (Winota): Fast, punishing hatebears stax into infinite combat (Kiki-Jiki + Combat Celebrant).
  - *The Copied Factory* (Inalla): Assembled infinite mana/ETB recursion loop (Dualcaster Mage + Ghostly Flicker).

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Mulligan Heuristics
- **Keep Criteria:** 3–4 lands, on-curve mana fixing, and an active engine piece (e.g., cheap cantrip for Alisaie/Alphinaud, discard enabler for Quintorius, early Knight/legend).
- **Mulligan Triggers:** Unplayable color screw, hands with purely reactive spells and no proactive engine, or high-curve threats with zero early setup.
- **Attitude:** Patient and disciplined. Relies on solid 36–37 land bases and does not gamble on high-risk 1-2 land hands.

### Sequencing & Combat Behavior
- **Sequencing Rigor:** Highly deliberate. Carefully calculates trigger orders (e.g. casting the cantrip first to open the second-spell slot for Alisaie's Dualcast discount; queuing ETBs before attacks).
- **Combat Posture:** Proactive board developer. Attacks early for chip damage or trigger activation (Winota, Balthier & Fran, Tifa), rarely holding back attackers unless facing an imminent lethal crackback.

### Threat Perception & Removal Priority
- **Primary Targets:** Fast infinite combos, stax pieces that freeze his specific engine (e.g., Rule of Law stopping his double-spell turns), and runaway card advantage engines (The One Ring).
- **Secondary Targets:** Value engines controlled by lto888 or reimaru.
- **Biases:** Tends to underestimate how threatening his own board appears to the rest of the table when Winota or Inalla are in the command zone.

### Table Talk & Politics
- **Voice & Tone:** Analytical, observant, friendly, mechanically descriptive. Often explains complex trigger stacks aloud to ensure clear board communication.
- **Deflection Strategy:** Deflects table heat by pointing out raw staple disparities: *"I'm just casting a cantrip to make a 1/1 token. Look at lto888 over there with a Turn 3 The One Ring and three open mana with Deadly Rollick."*
- **Reaction to Salt / Removal:** Respects clean, well-timed interaction. Can feel mildly disappointed if wiped out before an intricate engine gets to demonstrate its intended loop, but will adapt and rebuild.
