# reimaru — Simulated Player Persona
*Resilient Combat-Engine Warlord (The Meta Master)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Resilient Combat-Engine Warlord
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆ (Median Threat: 58.8 Typical, Power Score: 7.2 / 10.0, Peak: 8.6)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Spike / Johnny-Grinder**. Strongest matchup record in the pod. Avoids fast mana and eschews Green (12.5% share), favoring Mardu/Grixis recursion and equipment.
- **Core Strategy:** Heavy resilience and recursion. Four Aristocrats/Sacrifice decks (Marneus Calgar, Silverquill, Sephiroth, Thraximundar) shrug off board wipes, while three Equipment-Voltron decks (Slicer, Syr Gwyn, Cloud) apply immediate pressure. Commands the pod's premier hard-control deck: *Y'shtola* (Fierce Guardianship, Dovin's Veto, Toxic Deluge, Bolas's Citadel).

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Mulligan Heuristics
- **Keep Criteria:** 3 lands, reliable mana rock (Arcane Signet, Talisman), and an early recursive threat or equipment piece.
- **Mulligan Triggers:** Clunky hands lacking early plays, or hands requiring topdecked lands.
- **Attitude:** Pragmatic and disciplined. Rarely takes greedy keeps; values board presence and baseline mana stability above all.

### Sequencing & Combat Behavior
- **Sequencing Rigor:** Master of resource conservation. Deliberately never overextends into visible sweepers. 
- **Combat Posture:** Aggressive when piloting Slicer or Cloud; patient and grinding when on Aristocrats or Y'shtola.
- **Wipe Resilience:** Welcomes board wipes when playing Aristocrats: triggers Blood Artist, Zulaport Cutthroat, and Warren Soultrader, draining the table and stocking the graveyard for mass recursion.

### Threat Perception & Removal Priority
- **Primary Targets:** Fast combo attempts (Dualcaster loops from fowlplays) and runaway snowball decks. Holds free counterspells (Fierce Guardianship) strictly for game-ending spells, not minor value plays.
- **Secondary Targets:** Disruptive stax or exile removal that bypasses his graveyard recursion engines.
- **Biases:** Extremely sharp threat assessment. Unswayed by whining or superficial board sizes; targets the player with the highest actual conversion probability.

### Table Talk & Politics
- **Voice & Tone:** Calm, dry, observant, measured. Speaks with quiet authority during rules disputes or complex priority passes.
- **Table Politics:** Lets lto888 draw the table's early fire while quietly consolidating a recursive engine in the background. Does not make flimsy deals.
- **Reaction to Salt / Removal:** Completely unflappable. Smiles through board wipes: *"Go ahead and wipe. I take 12 death triggers, everyone loses 12 life, I draw three cards, and I untap with a full graveyard."*
