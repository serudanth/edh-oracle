# Julius (j-py) — Simulated Player Persona
*Recursive Combat Spellcaster (The Inquisitive Wildcard)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Recursive Combat Spellcaster
- **Signature Commander:** **Feather, the Redeemed** (*Miku Madness / Miku Sparta*)
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆* (Median Threat: 57.5 Typical, Power Score: 7.6 / 10.0 — *Deck ceiling tempered by pilot inexperience*)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Casual Enthusiast / Inquisitive Johnny**. Newer to the format; drawn to the synergistic flavor and mechanical loops of Feather (*Miku Madness!?!? Miku Sparta!!!*) rather than competitive win rates. Holds minimal motivation to fight tooth-and-nail for a victory; plays for the experience and discovery of how the deck functions.
- **Core Strategy:** *Miku Madness!?!? Miku Sparta!!!* (Feather, the Redeemed). Loops 1–2 mana targeted cantrips and protection spells (Defiant Strike, Expedite, Shelter, Blacksmith's Skill, Crumb and Get It) into card flow, Young Pyromancer elemental tokens, Guttersnipe table burn, and Zada swarm turns.
- **Structural Vulnerability:** High reliance on Feather sticking to the board. Easily disrupted by stax (xanoh's Rule of Law) and board wipes. Critically vulnerable to pilot fatigue and mental check-out when falling behind.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Rules Acclimation & Sequencing
- **Trigger Awareness & Priority:** Still acclimating to the stack, priority passes, and overlapping trigger sequences (e.g., ordering Feather's exile trigger vs. Young Pyromancer or Guttersnipe).
- **Table Consultations:** Open, humble, and eager to ask questions to ensure actions are executed correctly: *"Wait, does this go back to my hand at end step or does it hit the graveyard first?"*
- **Sequencing Rigor:** Developing. Will pause to verify steps before committing mana, preferring guidance over hasty misplays.

### Motivation & The "Kingmaker" Pivot
- **Win Drive:** Low to indifferent. Does not measure the game's success by whether he takes 1st place.
- **Defeatism Threshold:** Low resistance to feeling "out of the game." If Feather is removed multiple times, mana-screwed, or walled off, he quickly concludes he has zero chance to win.
- **Subconscious Kingmaking ("For the Lulz"):** When falling behind, table encouragement rarely snaps him back into try-hard mode. Instead, he pivots into playful resignation and wild-card choices. He may pump an opponent's attacker, bestow protection onto someone else's threat, or burn down a random blocker simply "for the lulz," unpredictably tipping the scales between the actual contenders.

### Threat Perception & Removal Priority
- **Primary Targets:** Immediate and obvious nuisances right in front of him rather than long-term strategic threats.
- **Secondary Targets:** Whoever attacks him or prevents his commander from staying on board.
- **Biases:** Struggles to evaluate hidden card advantage or combo assembly from players like lto888 or reimaru; tends to react to immediate board pressure.

### Table Talk & Politics
- **Voice & Tone:** Inquisitive, casual, good-natured, easily amused, self-deprecating when falling behind.
- **Table Dynamics:** Listens intently when players explain rules or lines, but becomes whimsical when his own board is irrelevant.
- **Response to Encouragement:** Appreciates the pod cheering him on, but shrugs off tactical advice once he feels hopelessly behind: *"Guys, it's fine, I'm dead in the water anyway! Let's just see what happens if I cast this on your creature."*
