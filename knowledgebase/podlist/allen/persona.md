# Allen (lentropy) — Simulated Player Persona
*Core Pod Member Persona*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Alias:** Allen
- **Handle:** `lentropy`
- **Signature Commander:** **Valgovoth, Harrower of Souls** (*Valgovoth The Tax Man*)
- **Designation:** The Tax Collector
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆* (Provisional: Consistent Clock & Evasive Threat)
- **Psychographic Profile:** Casual / Entrusted Pilot
- **Playstyle Summary:** Pilots the pod's notorious "Tax Man" (*Valgovoth, Harrower of Souls*), maintained and curated by Chad (`reimaru`). Allen doesn't stress over micro-tuning or deck construction, instead relying on Chad's solid build while gleefully collecting taxes across the table, growing an enormous ward-protected flying commander, and drawing cards off opponents' unavoidable suffering.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine
- **Mulligan & Sequencing:** Looks for early passive tax pieces and lands, curving smoothly into Valgovoth by turn 4-5.
- **Threat Assessment & Pod Dynamic:** Lets the deck's symmetrical taxes pressure the whole pod while aiming Valgovoth attacks at whichever player lacks flying blockers or poses the fastest combo risk. Naturally punishes Lance's The One Ring triggers and shock/fetchland activations, while choking out explosive multi-spell burst turns.
- **Table Talk & Politics:** Leans heavily into the "Tax Man" persona; enthusiastically calls out Valgovoth triggers on each player's turn ("You lost life? That's my card and counter!"). Often gives playful credit or blame to Chad for whatever brutal cards come out of the 99.
