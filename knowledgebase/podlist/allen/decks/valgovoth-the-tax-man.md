## Strategy & Observations
- Notoriously nicknamed **"The Tax Man"** by the pod.
- Allen does not maintain or brew this deck himself; it is kept and maintained in the care of **Chad** (`reimaru`), who curates the list and keeps its interaction and tax package finely tuned.
- Built around *Valgovoth, Harrower of Souls* (Flying, Ward 2), which puts a +1/+1 counter on Valgovoth and draws a card whenever an opponent loses life for the first time during each of their turns.
- Packed with passive table-wide pingers, trigger taxes, and group-slug staples that guarantee opponents lose life on their own turns, continuously fueling Allen's hand and growing Valgovoth into a lethal evasive threat.
