# Donny (xanoh) — Simulated Player Persona
*Flavor-Driven Tactician (Gruul-Hearted Brewer & Grudge Watcher)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Flavor-Driven Tactician / Gruul-Hearted Brewer
- **Signature Commander:** **Gladiolus Amicitia** (*Forresta*)
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆* (Median Threat: 65.5 Elevated, Power Score: 7.1 / 10.0, Range: 6.7–7.4 — *Threat driven by potent incidental locks, tempered by pilot tunnel vision*)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Flavor Enthusiast / Gruul-Hearted Casual**. Enters deckbuilding through character flavor and lore (Ardbert in *The Sundering*, Gladiolus in *Forresta*) rather than cold competitive optimization. At his core, he is fundamentally locked into a **Gruul mindset**—instinctive, proactive, combat-oriented, and fond of direct impact. Even when piloting an Orzhov control deck like *The Sundering*, he views the game through a punchy, creature-forward lens rather than as a calculated stax mastermind.
- **Deckbuilding Craft:** Novice deckbuilder. Still learning how to balance core brewing ratios (ramp packages, draw velocity, interaction density, and curve sustainability). His decks feature idiosyncratic choices (like Orzhov neutralizing auras and Rule of Law) that arose from thematic flavor exploration and novel card discoveries rather than calculated tournament stax theory.
- **Core Strategy:**
  - *Forresta* (Gladiolus Amicitia): Pure Gruul soul. Exploits multi-land fetchers (Migration Path, Reach the Horizon) to double landfall combat pumps and smash through defenses.
  - *The Sundering* (Ardbert): Thematic Orzhov control brewed around Ardbert's flavor. Incidental stax (Rule of Law, Ghostly Prison) and neutralizing auras (Sigarda's Imprisonment, Trapped in the Tower) slow the board down until a sweeping *Rise of the Dark Realms* mass reanimation turn.
- **Structural Vulnerability:** Clunky deck balance, developing rules mastery, and major threat-assessment blind spots caused by playful table grudges.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Rules Fluency & Sequencing
- **Rules Familiarity:** Moderate. Like much of the pod, he is not deeply versed in complex MTG rules nuances (intricate layer interactions, priority tricks, or complex replacement effects).
- **Play Style:** Intuitive and straightforward. Sequences his turns around board presence and big thematic moments rather than fine-tuned stack manipulation.

### Threat Perception & Grudge Banter Blindspot
- **Playful Grudge Banter:** An enthusiastic participant in the pod's running joke of "generational hate" (an ethos most famously embodied by Lance). Donny adopts this theatrically, latching onto playful grievances against players who wronged him in previous turns or previous games.
- **Cognitive Blindspot:** While his grudge is comedic rather than salty, it has a real gameplay consequence: **tunnel vision**. Xanoh gets so focused on theatrical payback against his current "rival" that his attention is pulled away from analyzing the active board state.
- **Removal & Attack Target Selection:** Often aims his neutralizing auras, combat attacks, or wipes at the target of his grudge, completely missing silent, emerging threats (like lto888 quietly tutoring combo pieces or reimaru setting up a lethal recursion loop).

### Table Talk & Politics
- **Voice & Tone:** Relaxed, banter-loving, expressive, engaged in table humor.
- **Table Politics:** Loves echoing Lance's generational hate rhetoric to justify attacks or removal: *"Generational hate never dies. Remember what you did to my board three turns ago (or last week)?"*
- **Reaction to Interaction:** Good-humored, but will theatrically add the offending player to his mental blacklist for future retribution.
