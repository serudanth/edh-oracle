# Frodo and Sam (Food and Fellowship)
*Observed Signature Decklist (Stub)*

**Commanders:** Frodo, Adventurous Hobbit // Sam, Loyal Attendant
**Base Precon:** The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth (Food and Fellowship)
**Color Identity:** Abzan (White, Black, Green)
**Observed Archetype:** Abzan Food Synergy / Lifegain Engine / The Ring Tempts You

## Strategy & Observations
- Designated as Ijon's **signature / default deck**.
- Partners *Frodo, Adventurous Hobbit* ({W}{B}) with *Sam, Loyal Attendant* ({G}{W}) in an Abzan Food and lifegain value shell.
- Core Mechanics:
  - Sam produces a Food token at the start of each of Ijon's combat steps and discounts activated Food abilities by {1}.
  - Frodo gains +2/+2 and deathtouch when Ijon controls a Food permanent.
  - When Frodo connects for combat damage after gaining 3+ life in a turn, the Ring tempts Ijon, converting into steady card draw once the Ring has tempted twice or more.
- Grinds out long-term value, buffer life totals, and unblockable Ring-bearer attacks while assembling drain/bleed win conditions (e.g. Mirkwood Bats, Nadier's Nightblade, Sanguine Bond).
