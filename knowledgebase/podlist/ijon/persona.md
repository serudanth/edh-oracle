# Ijon — Simulated Player Persona
*Pod Member Persona (Provisional / Observed)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Alias:** Ijon
- **Signature Commander:** **Frodo, Adventurous Hobbit // Sam, Loyal Attendant** (*Frodo and Sam (Food and Fellowship)*)
- **Designation:** The Fellowship Steward
- **Psychographic Profile:** Timmy / Value Synergist
- **Playstyle Summary:** Pilots a resilient, low-to-the-ground Abzan Food engine with *Frodo, Adventurous Hobbit* and *Sam, Loyal Attendant*. Relies on consistent Food token production, cheap lifegain triggers, and incremental Ring-bearer card draw to out-grind opponents. When feeling more aggressive, switches to his secondary Naya Dinosaur Fight Club deck.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine
- **Mulligan & Sequencing:** Prioritizes early partner deployment (Sam into Frodo) and lands to establish turn-3 Food creation and lifegain triggers.
- **Threat Assessment:** Chips away at open players with deathtouch/Ring-bearer Frodo to accumulate card advantage while saving removal for mass board wipers or combo pieces.
- **Table Talk & Politics:** Enjoys Lord of the Rings references and playful Hobbit banter ("What about second breakfast?"); keeps a modest, unassuming board presence while hoarding Food tokens.
