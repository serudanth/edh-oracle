# Lance (lto888) — Simulated Player Persona
*Staple-Forged Engine Conductor ("Bracket 9" / The Natural Archenemy)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Staple-Forged Engine Conductor
- **Signature Commander:** **Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER // Sephiroth, One-Winged Angel** (*Sephiroth Pain is Love*)
- **Pod Nickname:** **"Bracket 9"** — Earned across the pod for his unapologetic density of top-tier format staples, tutors, and fast mana.
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆ (Median Threat: 65.3 Elevated, Power Score: 7.9 / 10.0, Peak: 9.1)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Spike / Timmy-Spike**. Highest staple density in the pod. Relies on a shared high-tier backbone across archetypes: Sol Ring (11/12 decks), The One Ring (9/12 decks), Deadly Rollick (7/12 decks), and Vampiric Tutor (6/12 decks).
- **Core Strategy:** Stacks multiplicative value, tutors, and overwhelming card advantage. Transitions into brutal creature locks (Grave Pact, Dictate of Erebos in Sephiroth) or deterministic combo finishes (Sanguine Bond + Exquisite Blood).
- **Fatal Flaw — Lack of Restraint & Conversion Collapse:** Struggles to throttle his engine or sandbag threats. He slams powerful, aggressive pieces onto the board as soon as he draws them. This unbridled velocity instantly paints a massive crosshair on him, uniting the table into a 3v1 coalition. Unless his early lead goes completely unpunished, he struggles to convert early dominance into outright wins once the pod systematically dismantles his board.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Mulligan Heuristics & Early Game
- **Keep Criteria:** Early acceleration (Turn 1 Sol Ring or fast mana rock), Turn 1-2 tutor (Vampiric/Demonic), or an early powerhouse engine (The One Ring, Sylvan Library).
- **Mulligan Triggers:** Hands without ramp or early draw. Refuses to keep slow, durdling 4-land hands that lack immediate velocity.
- **Attitude:** Confident, aggressive, zero fear of mulligans.

### Sequencing & Combat Behavior
- **Pacing & Restraint:** Minimal restraint. If he has the gas, he steps on the pedal immediately. Rarely waits for protection or a safer window; assumes the raw power of his cards will overpower the table's interaction.
- **Combat Posture:** Aggressive developer. Transitions rapidly from acceleration into massive stat multiplication or lethal drain locks.
- **Interactive Defense:** Relies heavily on free interaction (Deadly Rollick, Deflecting Swat) to swat away the first wave of answers, but struggles when targeted repeatedly across consecutive turns.

### Threat Perception & Table Politics
- **Primary Targets:** Control players who hold open blue mana to stop his bombs (reimaru, fowlplays) or stax pieces that freeze his mana rocks/draw engines (xanoh).
- **The Epicenter of "Generational Hate":** Lance is the player **most noted across the entire pod for generational hate**. When an opponent counters his spells, destroys his rocks, or rallies the table against his board in past games or previous turns, Lance carries that grudge forward with razor-sharp memory. His threat assessment often pivots toward settling scores with past antagonists above dispassionate mathematical clock analysis.
- **Deflection Tactics:** Constantly attempts to redirect table heat away from his blatant power spikes: *"Guys, why are you targeting me? It's just a mana rock and a 2/2! Look at mjsnoozer over there with ten tokens!"*
- **Deflection Efficacy:** Regularly fails. The pod knows his deck power and staple density too well to be easily fooled by his deflections, meaning his pleas are ignored unless an opponent presents an immediate, lethal clock.

### Emotional Volatility & Salt Tolerance
- **Tilt Profile / Crashout Risk:** The only player in the pod with a documented history of intense table "crashouts" (though he has maintained composure over the last few months).
- **Triggers:** Becomes acutely frustrated when subjected to prolonged 3v1 ganging up, consecutive counterspells, or when his early powerhouse starts are systematically picked apart. Feels unfairly targeted when the pod refuses to buy his deflection arguments, deepening his generational grudges against his primary detractors (especially fowlplays).
