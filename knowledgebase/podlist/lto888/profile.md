## Overview
LTO888 builds a wide spread of two- and three-color decks around synergy-driven tribal/subtheme shells (squirrels, cats-and-dogs, ninjutsu, enchantress) rather than a single repeated archetype. Black is the one color present in every color combination shy of pure mono-green, and roughly half the decks (5 of 11) are built around Final Fantasy or Avatar: The Last Airbender commanders, with Universes Beyond cards from FF, ATLA, Marvel, and D&D/BG3 turning up even in decks with no crossover theme. Card pools lean on efficient, well-known staples (The One Ring, Vampiric Tutor, Fierce Guardianship, Sol Ring) rather than budget or janky choices, putting most lists around a self-tagged Archidekt bracket 4 where noted.

## Color & Archetype Tendencies
- Black appears in 6 of 11 decks (the most of any color), followed by white, blue, and red tied at 5, and green at 4 — no color is avoided, but green is the least represented.
- Color-combo split: 2 mono-color decks (mono-G Squirrels, mono-B Sephiroth), 4 two-color decks (WR, WG, UR, UB), 5 three-color decks (UBG, WRG, UBR, WBR, WUB). No four- or five-color builds.
- Blue+black together anchors 4 of 11 decks (Yuriko UB, Maralen UBG, Azula UBR, Y'shtola WUB), suggesting a comfort pairing even though neither color individually dominates the spread.
- Recognizable archetypes recur rather than repeat verbatim: go-wide tokens/+1-1 counters (Squirrels, Bau Bau Nyaa — both run the same Doubling Season + Parallel Lives + Beastmaster Ascension package on different commanders), aristocrats/sacrifice (Sephiroth, with a lighter sac subtheme in Celes), spellslinger/instant-heavy (Azula at 39 instants, Y'shtola at 25, Prismari Artistry's magecraft shell), reanimator/recursion (Celes, plus a reanimator subpackage inside Sephiroth), tribal (Squirrels, Bau Bau Nyaa cats-and-dogs, Yuriko ninjutsu), enchantress (Yuna), and voltron/equipment with extra combats (Lightning).
- Squirrels and Bau Bau Nyaa are effectively sibling builds: different commanders and tribes, but the same "stack the doubling effects, go wide, clamp for cards" skeleton (Doubling Season, Parallel Lives, Beastmaster Ascension, Skullclamp, Second Harvest, Shamanic Revelation all shared).

## Power Level & Construction Habits
- Only 3 of 11 decks note an Archidekt bracket in their Notes section (Squirrels, Sephiroth, Y'shtola) — all three are bracket 4, suggesting a consistent power target even where it isn't logged.
- The One Ring appears in 8 of 11 decks and Sol Ring in 10 of 11 (missing only from Maralen) — near-universal reliance on the same top-tier ramp/value staples regardless of archetype.
- Deadly Rollick shows up in 6 of 11 decks and Vampiric Tutor in 5 — a strong "free interaction + tutor" habit in any deck that can support black.
- Land counts run 33–38, mostly clustering at 37–38 (norm-consistent), with two notable outliers on the low end: Y'shtola at 33 lands (low-curve control/spellslinger) and Yuriko at 34 lands (cheap evasive ninjutsu aggro).
- Fixing scales with color count and gameplan rather than color count alone: mono-color and two-color aggressive decks (Squirrels, Sephiroth, Lightning, Yuriko) stay 50–90% basics, while three-color control/value decks get fixing-heavy — Y'shtola (Esper control) runs only 7 basics out of 33 lands, and Celes (Mardu) only 12 of 37.
- No fast-mana outliers (no Mana Crypt, Mox, or similar) and no repeated infinite-combo callouts across decks — power sits in efficient-staples-plus-tutors territory rather than cEDH-adjacent speed.
- Strict one-copy singleton discipline throughout, as expected for Commander; no near-duplicate effects stacked beyond the intentional doubling-effects package noted above.

## Notable Patterns
- Five of 11 commanders are Universes Beyond crossover characters — three from Final Fantasy (Sephiroth, Celes, Y'shtola all lean FFXIV-flavored; Lightning and Yuna also FF) and one from Avatar: The Last Airbender (Azula) — making UB commanders roughly half the sample, not an occasional pick.
- UB cards leak into decks that aren't thematically UB at all: the mono-green Squirrels deck runs Ba Sing Se (ATLA land), Spider-Ham Peter Porker and Undercover Skrull (Marvel), and Peregrin Took (LOTR) purely for squirrel-tribal or mana-fixing utility. The Boros equipment deck runs Bruenor Battlehammer, Merry Esquire of Rohan (LOTR), and Karlach, Fury of Avernus (BG3/D&D). This suggests LTO888 treats crossover cards as a card pool to mine for mechanical fit rather than restricting them to matching-theme decks.
- Wan Shi Tong, Librarian (an ATLA card) appears in both the ATLA-themed Azula deck and the FF-themed Y'shtola deck — a specific crossover card reused across two unrelated IP themes.
- Sephiroth, Fabled SOLDIER (the commander of one deck) also shows up as a maindeck card inside the Celes deck — a commander-as-inclusion crossover between two of LTO888's own lists.
- Yuna's enchantress build folds the Final Fantasy "Summon" Saga cycle (Bahamut, Choco/Mog, Fenrir, Good King Mog XII, Knights of Round, Magus Sisters, Primal Garuda, Titan, Yojimbo, plus Esper Origins) directly into the enchantment-matters shell — since Sagas are enchantments, the crossover subtheme and the mechanical archetype reinforce each other rather than sitting side by side.
- Treno, Dark City (an FF IX-flavored land) appears in two unrelated decks (Azula, Yuriko), and FF-specific utility lands (Midgar, Susur Secundi) show up even in the mono-black Sephiroth list — crossover picks extend into the manabase, not just the spell lines.
- Azula's list is an outlier on instant density (39 instants against 22 creatures) and on land count (35, the second-lowest in the sample) — the most spell-dominant, lowest-creature-count build outside of Y'shtola's dedicated control shell.

### Commander Spellbook combo check (Azula)
- Fire Lord Azula's 39-instant, spell-dominant build lines up with real, well-documented infinite combos on Commander Spellbook, not just a "spell-heavy" flavor read. Most notably **Fire Lord Azula + Narset's Reversal + Storm-Kiln Artist** — a loop producing infinite Treasures, infinite colored mana, infinite copies of the cast instant, and infinite storm count, used in over 11,000 decks per EDHREC's combo tracker. Multiple other Azula + Narset's Reversal lines (with Frantic Search, Vedalken Orrery, Hypersonic Dragon, Tidal Barracuda, etc.) produce infinite turns or infinite mana/draw. Whether LTO888's specific 99 includes Narset's Reversal wasn't re-checked against the decklist file here, but the commander itself is a known combo enabler — worth flagging as the one deck in the sample most likely to have an actual infinite line available, consistent with the profile's read.

### Crossover card rulings check
- **Wan Shi Tong, Librarian** (`{X}{U}{U}` Legendary Creature — Bird Spirit): confirmed flash/flying/vigilance ETB-counters-and-draw creature with a second ability that triggers off *opponents* searching *their own* library (ruling: does not trigger on searching an opponent's library, or on watching another player search a third player's library). Consistent with its use as a value/card-advantage piece rather than a combo enabler in both the Azula and Y'shtola lists.
- **Treno, Dark City**: confirmed as a Land — Town that enters tapped, taps for {U} or {B}. Straightforward UB fixing/utility land, confirming the profile's read of it as a crossover pick for the manabase rather than for any special ability.
- **FF "Summon" Saga cycle** (Bahamut, Fenrir, etc.): confirmed via Scryfall/Wizards release notes as Magic's first-ever **Enchantment Creature — Saga** type — genuinely both a Saga and a creature simultaneously, with lore-counter chapters like ordinary Sagas (sacrificed after the final chapter) but able to attack, block, wear equipment, etc. while on the battlefield. This confirms the profile's claim that these cards are mechanically enchantments (triggering enchantress payoffs) while also functioning as creatures/threats in Yuna's shell — not just flavor overlap.
