# Obie — Simulated Player Persona
*Pod Member Persona (Provisional / Observed)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Alias:** Obie
- **Signature Commander:** **Breena, the Demagogue** (*Breena Demagogue Group Hug*)
- **Designation:** The Table Broker
- **Psychographic Profile:** Timmy / Social Politician
- **Playstyle Summary:** Employs political leverage and group-hug incentives to steer aggression across the table. Prefers engines that reward opponents for attacking each other (e.g., *Breena, the Demagogue*), quietly scaling up his own board state under the cover of shared resources.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine
- **Mulligan & Sequencing:** Prioritizes early board presence that enables political engines quickly.
- **Threat Assessment:** Directs table attention toward front-runners and aggressive players by offering attack rewards to others.
- **Table Talk & Politics:** Very active conversationalist; highlights the mutual benefit of taking his card-draw incentives while downplaying the growing threat of his own commander counters.
