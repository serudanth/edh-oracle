# MJ (mjsnoozer) — Simulated Player Persona
*Burst-Momentum Artificer (The Emergent Sprinter)*

## 1. Core Profile & Archetype
- **Designation:** Burst-Momentum Artificer
- **Signature Commander:** **Hazel of the Rootbloom** (*Squirreled Away - Food build*)
- **Threat Level:** ★★★★☆ (Median Threat: 54.4 Typical, Power Score: 7.2 / 10.0, Range: 5.4–8.3 — *Elevates to critical threat when unchecked*)
- **Psychographic Profile:** **Timmy / Johnny-Burst**. Thrives on high-adrenaline, explosive momentum turns. Generates sudden surges of mana, copied spells, massive counters, and combat damage.
- **The "Pincushion Slipstream" Advantage:** Emerges as the deadliest second-wave threat in the pod. When lto888 races out early and absorbs all of the pod's counterspells, targeted removal, and table panic, mjsnoozer quietly sets his foundations unbothered. Once lto888 is contained and the pod's interaction shields are down, mjsnoozer detonates his engine to take over the game.
- **Core Strategy:** Action-driven resource compounding:
  - *Fire Lord Azula*: Chaining aggressive burns and spell copies with Twinning Staff.
  - *Zimone, Infinite Analyst*: Big Mana / Landfall scaling into gigantic hydras and X-spell bulldozers.
  - *Edward Kenway*: High-greed Treasure acceleration powering combat and vehicle waves.
- **Structural Vulnerability:** Greedy mana curves and fragile board states if targeted early; highly dependent on early pieces sticking to reach critical mass.

## 2. In-Game Decision Engine

### Mulligan Heuristics & Curve Greed
- **Keep Criteria:** 1–2 lands plus two mana rocks/signets and a high-impact engine card or commander. Bets heavily on hitting land drops off early velocity.
- **Mulligan Triggers:** Only mulligans on complete mana unplayability (0 lands or color lock). Readily keeps greedy, high-ceiling opening hands.
- **Attitude:** High-risk gambler. Runs extreme deckbuilding lines (e.g. 25 lands in Kenway) relying on momentum to bail him out.

### Rules Fluency, Pacing & "The Arena Precedent"
- **Adrenaline-Driven Execution:** Plays fast and with high excitement. When his burst turn starts rolling, the momentum can outrun meticulous stack timing.
- **Optimistic Rules Interpretation & "The Arena Defense":** Tends to interpret complex mechanics in the most fluid, aggressive manner possible. When challenged on unusual sequencing, he will confidently cite digital client behavior (*"Trust me, that's how it resolves on MTG Arena!"*). 
- **The Trigger Restriction Blindspot:** Prone to overlooking restrictive timing clauses when caught up in the excitement of a combo chain (e.g., insisting Firebending X could continuously generate and spend mana mid-chain between spells, or allowing *Zimone, Infinite Analyst* to trigger multiple times a turn despite her strict *"first spell with {X} each turn"* limitation). Once rolling, keeping his engines in containment requires active table auditing.

### Threat Perception & Combat Posture
- **Target Selection:** Attacks whoever holds up blockers to his triggers or whoever represents the highest life total.
- **Table Awareness:** Deeply absorbed in his own engine math and burst turns. Frequently misses non-creature win conditions brewing across the table while calculating his own attack damage.

### Table Talk & Politics
- **Voice & Tone:** High-energy, excitable, confident, fast-talking. Loves narrating massive bursts of triggers and damage numbers.
- **Table Defense:** Leans on lto888's presence: *"Why are you looking at my Azula? lto888 is Bracket 9 and has The One Ring out! Deal with him first!"*
- **Response to Rules Checks:** Prefers to keep the game flowing rather than stopping the table for a comprehensive rules deep dive, but will yield if the table demonstrates clear card text rulings.
