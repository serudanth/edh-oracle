## Power Level & Construction Habits
- None of the active Archidekt imports has a bracket number recorded — power level is derived from card choices and the deterministic analyzer engine (Brackets 2–3 across all active decks).
- Land counts sit below the 36-40 norm across several lists: Cloud 32, Azula 35, Hazel 37, Zimone 37, Kenway 25 (extreme outlier, propped up by Sol Ring + 3 guild Signets + 3 Talismans + Chromatic Lantern).
- Zimone X Bulldozer (Power Score 7.2, Bracket 3, Big Mana / Landfall) channels big mana and flash discount triggers into 44 X-cost spells: hydras (Benevolent Hydra, Goldvein Hydra, Primordial Hydra, Steelbane Hydra, Hydroid Krasis), counter multipliers (Hardened Scales, Innkeeper's Talent, The Ozolith, Ozolith, the Shattered Spire, Unbound Flourishing), and explosive X-spells (Blue Sun's Zenith, Doppelgang, Finale of Revelation, Hurricane, Spinning Wheel Kick). Printed average CMC sits at 1.79 due to rule 107.3f, with an operational effective curve of 3.93 CMC (detailed in `knowledgebase/research/2026-09-17-zimone-x-bulldozer-deck-reanalysis.md`).
- Azula carries real storm/combo-adjacent pieces (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Mizzix's Mastery, Grapeshot, Isochron Scepter, Twinning Staff) inherited from the Stella Lee build, but as currently constructed this reads as a strong non-infinite value/storm-adjacent package rather than a live cEDH combo line — see the Commander Spellbook check below, which found no assemblable infinite combo in the deck as built. Twinning Staff is worth flagging as a build-around specifically for *this* commander though: it also triggers off Azula's own attack-trigger copy, not just spells copied by casting, so a resolved Twinning Staff turns her "copy the spell" into "copy it twice" automatically.
- Cloud and Hazel read as more conventional midrange (equipment-voltron-but-wide, and token/aristocrats value respectively), with ramp spells substituting for a full land count rather than fast mana.
- Foils are concentrated in Cloud (Cloud, Planet's Champion; Buster Sword; Mantle of the Ancients; Thornspire Verge) and Azula (Bria, Riptide Rogue; Enduring Curiosity) — no foils recorded in Hazel, Kenway, or Zimone, suggesting Cloud and Azula remain the most personally-invested active builds.

## Notable Patterns
- The active decks all seek resource generation through action rather than passive control: attacks draw cards or create Treasures, spells are copied, tokens are multiplied, and counters are scaled. This gives the portfolio a consistent preference for momentum and visible board development.
- The five lists occupy different engine spaces: Azula and Cloud are payoff-first constructions, Hazel goes wide with tokens, Kenway builds Treasure velocity, and Zimone scales vertically and horizontally through X-costs and +1/+1 counters.
- Mana is treated as a constraint to solve with artifacts, green ramp, and cost reduction. The approach supports explosive turns, but the low land totals, especially Kenway's 25, make missed land drops more punishing than in a conventional midrange portfolio.
- The retired Stella Lee list matters as construction history, not as a separate pattern: Azula inherited its spell core. The active decks therefore show adaptation around proven proactive engines more than broad experimentation with control or combo.
