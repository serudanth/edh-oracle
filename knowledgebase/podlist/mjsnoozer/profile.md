## Overview
mjsnoozer's five decks cluster around red-based multicolor shells (Grixis/Izzet appear in four of five) built on Universes Beyond commanders — Final Fantasy, Assassin's Creed, and Avatar: The Last Airbender headline three of the five decks, and crossover product shows up even inside the non-UB-commander builds. Two decks (Azula, Stella Lee) share a near-identical UR/UBR spellslinger-storm shell down to specific combo pieces, suggesting a repeatable "signature" archetype the player returns to. Land counts run below the 36-40 norm in every deck, compensated with varying degrees of rock-based and green ramp-based fixing rather than basics.

## Color & Archetype Tendencies
- Red appears in 4 of 5 decks (Cloud, Azula, Stella Lee, Edward Kenway); only the food/squirrels deck (Hazel, B/G) skips it entirely.
- Blue and black each appear in 3 of 5 decks, always together with red (Azula and Kenway are both UBR/Grixis; Stella Lee is UR).
- White and green are the least-used colors — white appears only in the Cloud deck (Naya, W/R/G), green only in Cloud and the Hazel food deck.
- No mono-color or 5-color decks; nothing pairs W with B, or leaves out red while keeping U/B.
- Two distinct archetype lanes recur: spellslinger/storm-adjacent Izzet-Grixis decks (Azula, Stella Lee) and vehicle/equipment-or-treasure decks with a UB tribal skin (Cloud's equipment voltron, Kenway's pirates/treasure/vehicles).
- The Hazel food/squirrel deck is the outlier — the only deck without a UB-commander anchor, and the only one leaning aristocrats/tokens (Zulaport Cutthroat, Skullclamp, Deadly Dispute, Chatterstorm) rather than spells-matter or equipment.

## Power Level & Construction Habits
- None of the five Archidekt imports have a bracket number recorded in their Notes section (unlike some pod-mates' files, e.g. reimaru's Moxfield import notes "bracket: 2") — power level has to be inferred from card choices rather than a stated bracket.
- Land counts sit below the 36-40 norm across the board: Cloud 32, Azula 35, Stella Lee 37, Hazel/food 37, Edward Kenway 25. Kenway's 25 lands is the extreme outlier, propped up by a heavy rock package (Sol Ring, 3 guild Signets, 3 Talismans, Chromatic Lantern).
- Azula and Stella Lee both carry genuine storm/combo pieces (Dark Ritual, Seething Song, Mizzix's Mastery, Grapeshot, Isochron Scepter/Twinning Staff in Azula) — these two read as the highest-power builds in the set, likely low-to-mid cEDH-adjacent rather than pure casual.
- Cloud and Hazel read as more conventional midrange (voltron-equipment and aristocrats/value, respectively) with ramp spells substituting for a full land base rather than fast-mana combo pieces.
- Foils are concentrated in exactly two decks — Cloud (Cloud Planet's Champion, Buster Sword, Mantle of the Ancients, Thornspire Verge) and Azula (Bria Riptide Rogue, Enduring Curiosity) — suggesting these are the player's most personally invested builds; Stella Lee, Hazel, and Kenway have none.

## Notable Patterns
- Azula and Stella Lee are effectively the same combo shell wearing two different commanders: both run Archmage Emeritus, Storm-Kiln Artist, Goblin Electromancer, Guttersnipe, Veyran Voice of Duality, Seething Song, Grapeshot, Mizzix's Mastery, Galvanic Iteration, and the Preordain/Ponder/Opt cantrip suite. This is the clearest "signature build" in the profile.
- mjsnoozer's UB commander picks pull in extra crossover product beyond the commander itself: the Cloud deck adds Assassin's Creed (Excalibur, Sword of Eden) and Fallout (Pip-Boy 3000, Pre-War Formalwear) cards alongside its Final Fantasy core; the Azula deck is built entirely around an Avatar: The Last Airbender sub-theme (Ty Lee, Wan Shi Tong, Fire Nation Turret/Occupation/Palace/Drill, Realm of Koh, The Rise of Sozin // Fire Lord Sozin) plus a Final Fantasy card (Vivi Ornitier); the Kenway deck is a full Assassin's Creed cast (Adéwalé, Cosima // The Omenkeel, Adrestia, Crystal Skull, Isu Spyglass, Jackdaw, Abstergo Entertainment). Even the one non-UB-anchored deck (Hazel/food) includes an LOTR crossover land (The Shire).
- Cross-checked against the rest of the pod: reimaru also runs a Cloud commander (Cloud, Midgar Mercenary, mono-W) and independently landed on the same equipment-voltron shell — Buster Sword, Ultima Weapon, Genji Glove, Conqueror's Flail, Mantle of the Ancients, Sigarda's Aid, Codsworth, Danitha Capashen, Puresteel Paladin, Sram Senior Edificer, and Cid Freeflier Pilot all appear in both lists. LTO888 also runs Fire Lord Azula and converges on much of the same core (Archmage Emeritus, Fire Sages, Firebending Student, Goblin Electromancer, Guttersnipe, Storm-Kiln Artist, Torrential Gearhulk, Wan Shi Tong, Bender's Waterskin, Sol Ring). In both cases the overlap looks like the commander's build space being narrow/well-defined rather than direct copying.
- MDFC/back-face cards show up more than usual, concentrated in Azula (Fell the Profane // Fell Mire, Sink into Stupor // Soporific Springs, Waterlogged Teachings // Inundated Archive, The Rise of Sozin // Fire Lord Sozin, Lindblum // Mage Siege) with one apiece in Cloud (Ishgard // Faith & Grief) and Kenway (Storm the Vault // Vault of Catlacan, Cosima // The Omenkeel).
- Fixing philosophy is inconsistent between decks: Azula skips Arcane Signet entirely in favor of three guild Signets (Dimir/Izzet/Rakdos), while Cloud, Stella Lee, Hazel, and Kenway all include Arcane Signet; Command Tower only appears in Stella Lee and Kenway, absent from the other three.
- Singleton discipline holds throughout — no duplicate nonbasic cards found in any of the five lists.

**Commander Spellbook — is the Azula/Stella Lee shell an actual infinite combo?**
- Confirmed named combo: **Storm-Kiln Artist + Seething Song + Reiterate** (infinite mana, infinite Treasures, infinite storm count/magecraft triggers) — but **neither deck runs Reiterate**, so this line isn't assemblable as built in either list.
- Confirmed named combo: **Veyran, Voice of Duality + Storm-Kiln Artist + View from Above** (infinite magecraft triggers/mana) — **neither deck runs View from Above**.
- Confirmed named combo: **Isochron Scepter + Dramatic Reversal** (classic infinite mana with ≥3 mana of untapped rocks) — the pieces are split across the two decks: Azula runs Isochron Scepter but not Dramatic Reversal; Stella Lee runs Dramatic Reversal but not Isochron Scepter. Not assemblable within either single deck, though notable that mjsnoozer owns both halves across the pair.
- Mizzix's Mastery's only cataloged Commander Spellbook combo is with Worldfire (near-infinite lifeloss/mill); Worldfire is in neither list, so Mizzix's Mastery functions as a big spell-recursion payoff here, not a combo piece.
- **Verdict**: the profile's "same combo shell" claim holds as an archetype/synergy claim (EDHREC independently tags both as Storm/Spellslinger with near-identical high-synergy card pools), but Commander Spellbook does not confirm a complete infinite combo in either decklist as currently built — each deck is one specific card away (Reiterate, View from Above, or the other's Isochron Scepter/Dramatic Reversal half) from a cataloged infinite line. As built, this reads as a strong non-infinite storm/spellslinger package rather than a live cEDH combo engine.
