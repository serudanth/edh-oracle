## Purpose & Baseline Role

These decks and profile are maintained in the repository solely as **authoritative cEDH baseline / control references**. They demonstrate what uncompromised, tournament-grade cEDH lists look like across key architectural dimensions:

- **Ultra-Compact Mana Curves:** 1.92–1.96 average CMC with only 26–28 lands, sustained by 3–7 fast mana rocks and dorks.
- **Extreme Format Staples Density:** 20–23 CFP Game Changers (The One Ring, Rhystic Study, Mystic Remora, Esper Sentinel, Orcish Bowmasters, Force of Will, Fierce Guardianship, Mindbreak Trap, Smothering Tithe, Ad Nauseam, Underworld Breach, Necropotence, etc.).
- **Tutor Saturation:** 14–16 deterministic tutors (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Mystical Tutor, Enlightened Tutor, Imperial Seal, Gamble, Intuition, etc.) ensuring turn-2 to turn-3 win attempt readiness.
- **Compact Infinite Win Conditions:**
  - **Blue Farm:** Underworld Breach + Brain Freeze + Lion's Eye Diamond / Lotus Petal storm loop, Thassa's Oracle + Tainted Pact / Demonic Consultation.
  - **TnT:** Thassa's Oracle + Tainted Pact / Demonic Consultation, Devoted Druid loops with Thrasios library draw.

## Pod Comparison Utility

When evaluating high-power pod lists (e.g. Lance's *Sephiroth / Pain is Love*, Julius's *Miku Madness*, or Charles's *The Rush*), these lists provide calibrated anchors to evaluate:
1. **Bracket 4 (High-Power Casual) vs. Bracket 5 (cEDH):** Demonstrating the stark divide between synergistic casual power (CMC 2.5–3.2, 33–36 lands, 2–5 tutors) and tournament efficiency.
2. **Interaction Density:** Illustrating the baseline requirement of free countermagic and stack interaction needed to survive a turn-2/3 win attempt in open cEDH.
3. **Theorycrafting Benchmark:** Serving as the direct reference model for Charles's *The Cheap Farm* / *The Budget Farm* theorycrafting builds ([`theorycrafting/blue-farm/`](file:///mnt/Hydaelyn/Azem/dev/edh-oracle/knowledgebase/theorycrafting/blue-farm/)).
