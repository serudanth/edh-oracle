## Methodology / Caveats

- Pricing is TCGplayer market price on flagged high-value repeat cards only (The One Ring, Smothering Tithe, Bolas's Citadel, Doubling Season, Skullclamp, Rhystic Study) — not a full per-card appraisal of every list.
- Archetype labels are one-line summaries of the deck's actual game plan as read from its 99, not EDHREC's auto-tag.
- Re-run this audit after any significant list changes — most notably fowlplays/The Best of Friends, which is mid-rebuild.
- The Crystal Braves (fowlplays) added 2026-08-22: Alisaie Leveilleur // Alphinaud Leveilleur partner pair (Azorius, WU), built around triggering "second spell each turn" payoffs (Alisaie's Dualcast cost reduction, Alphinaud's Eukrasia draw), layered with anthem effects and go-wide tokens. Bracket not yet assigned — no combo package was evident in the list.
- xanoh update 2026-08-22, pulled from Archidekt: Forresta (archidekt.com/decks/21400503) was last edited 2026-08-20 — re-checked category mix (Ramp 22, Landfall 7, Removal 5) and it's still the same Gruul landfall/ramp-stompy shell, no archetype change. Added a second deck, The Sundering (archidekt.com/decks/24557905, last edited 2026-07-31), commanded by Ardbert, Warrior of Darkness (WB) — note this is a different build from fowlplays' Ardbert deck ("The Warrior of Darkness," legends-matter); xanoh's list is removal-heavy (18) with protection (11), recursion (6), stax pieces (Rule of Law), drain, and discard — read as Orzhov control/stax rather than tribal. Neither deck has an EDH bracket set on Archidekt, so both are logged as "—" pending manual assignment.
