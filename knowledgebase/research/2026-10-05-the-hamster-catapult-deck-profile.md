## Overview

**The Hamster Catapult** is an aggressive Gruul (RG) Aggro-Stompy, +1/+1 Counter Scaling, and Ballistic Fling-Burn deck commanded by **Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes** (Archidekt Bracket 2/3, calibrated performance 7.3). Departing from traditional battlecruiser stompy decks that rely on slow, gradual creature deployment, the list operates as a high-velocity kinetic engine that converts command-zone card advantage into massive trampling combat pressure and targeted direct-damage burn:

1. **The Command Zone Hamster Velocity Loop:** A self-sustaining two-turn engine loop that produces a recurring hasty, trampling threat, targeted removal, and massive burst card draw directly from the command zone without expending nonland cards from hand.
2. **Exponential Counter Compounding & Damage Duplication:** Scales Boo and companion beaters well beyond standard stat curves through exponential counter doublers (**Sazh Katzroy**), damage multipliers (**Raphael, the Muscle**), passive counter burn (**Casey Jones, Back Alley Brute**), and kinetic death batteries (**Death's Presence**).
3. **The "Threaten-and-Catapult" Theft & Removal Engine:** Weaponizes temporary theft spells (**Act of Treason**, **Involuntary Employment**, **Unexpected Request**) to steal opposing blockers and commanders, attack with them, and sacrifice them to Minsc's `−2` or **Fling**—turning temporary theft into permanent removal and face burn.
4. **Overrun Vectors & Ballistic Direct-Damage Finishers:** Closes stalled boards rapidly via mass infect (**Triumph of the Hordes**), exponential mana sinks (**Kessig Wolf Run** with **Nyxbloom Ancient**), and trample-damage token cascades (**Quartzwood Crasher**).

## Mechanical Architecture

### 1. The Command Zone Kinetic Engine (Minsc & Boo)
The engine is commanded by **Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes** (`{2}{R}{G}`, Starting Loyalty: 3):

* **Passive ETB & Upkeep Trigger:**
  * *"When Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes enters the battlefield and at the beginning of your upkeep, you may create Boo, a legendary 1/1 red Hamster creature token with trample and haste."*
  * Ensures a resilient, free combat and sacrifice token is generated every turn cycle without mana investment.
* **`+1` Loyalty Ability:**
  * *"Put three +1/+1 counters on up to one target creature with trample or haste."*
  * Automatically targets Boo (or companion beaters like *Wilson, Refined Grizzly*, *Red XIII*, or *Quaketusk Boar*), immediately turning a 1/1 token into a 4/4 hasty trampler on curve.
* **`−2` Loyalty Ability:**
  * *"Sacrifice a creature. When you do, Minsc & Boo deals X damage to any target, where X is that creature's power. If the sacrificed creature was a Hamster, draw X cards."*
  * Acts as a scalable, command-zone removal spell, direct face burn, and massive card draw engine in a single loyalty activation.

---

### 2. Counter Compounding & Kinetic Multipliers
The 99 is tailored to accelerate counter placement and compound the damage dealt by creatures with counters:

* **Sazh Katzroy** (`{2}{R}`, 2/2 Pilot):
  * Whenever Sazh attacks, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature, **then double the number of +1/+1 counters on that creature**.
  * Chained onto a Boo with three counters from Minsc, Sazh elevates Boo to an 8-counter, **9/9 Trample Haste** monster on Turn 5.
* **Raphael, the Muscle** (`{2}{R}{G}`, 4/3):
  * *"Double all damage that creatures you control with counters on them would deal."*
  * Applies to both combat damage and ability/fling damage. A 7/7 Boo swings for **14 trample damage**, and the subsequent `−2` fling deals **14 direct damage**!
* **Casey Jones, Back Alley Brute** (`{2}{R}`, 3/3):
  * *"Whenever you put one or more +1/+1 counters on a creature you control, Casey Jones deals that much damage to target opponent."*
  * Activating Minsc & Boo's `+1` automatically deals **3 direct damage to an opponent's face**. Multi-counter spells like **Biogenic Upgrade** or attacks from **Leatherhead, Iron Gator** trigger simultaneous burn bursts.
* **Death's Presence** (`{5}{G}`):
  * *"Whenever a creature you control dies, put X +1/+1 counters on target creature you control, where X is its power."*
  * Serves as a kinetic storage battery: sacrificing an 8/8 Boo to Minsc's `−2` deals 8 damage, draws 8 cards, and immediately deposits **eight +1/+1 counters** onto another creature (**Wilson**, **Mowu**, or **Halana and Alena**).
* **Bone Sabres** (`{2}{G}`, Equip `{2}`):
  * Places four +1/+1 counters on the equipped creature whenever it attacks, rapidly growing any creature into a lethal missile.
* **Halana and Alena, Partners** (`{2}{R}{G}`, 2/3 First Strike, Reach):
  * At the beginning of combat on your turn, places +1/+1 counters equal to their power on another creature and grants it haste.
* **Mowu, Loyal Companion** (`{3}{G}`, 3/3 Trample, Vigilance):
  * Adds an additional +1/+1 counter whenever counters are placed on it, compounding Minsc's `+1` into four counters.

### 3. The "Threaten-and-Catapult" Theft & Removal Engine
The deck runs a dedicated package of temporary theft sorceries:
* **Act of Treason** (`{2}{R}`)
* **Goatnap** (`{2}{R}`)
* **Involuntary Employment** (`{3}{R}`, creates a Treasure token)
* **Portent of Betrayal** (`{3}{R}`, scries 1)
* **Unexpected Request** (`{2}{R}`, attaches equipment for free)

#### Tactical Loop
1. **Steal Key Threats:** Take control of an opponent's commander, indestructible blocker, or highest-power threat for 3 mana.
2. **Combat Pressure:** Swing at an opponent with their own hasted creature.
3. **Permanent Removal via Catapult:** In the postcombat main phase, sacrifice the stolen creature to Minsc & Boo's `−2` (or instant-speed **Fling** / **Special Move**). The opponent's creature is permanently removed to their graveyard/command zone, and its power is dealt as burn to any target.
4. **The Amorphous Axe Interaction:**
   * **Amorphous Axe** (`{2}`, Equip `{3}`) grants +3/+0 and gives the equipped creature **all creature types**.
   * Attaching Amorphous Axe to a stolen creature (or attaching it for free via *Unexpected Request*) turns the stolen creature into a **Hamster**!
   * Sacrificing the stolen creature to Minsc's `−2` deals its power as damage **and draws cards equal to its power**.

### 4. Card Advantage, Mana Velocity, & Ramp Infrastructure
To maintain a high casting cadence and ensure Minsc & Boo lands early:

* **Command Zone Card Draw:** Minsc & Boo draws 4 to 8 cards every other turn cycle, serving as the deck's primary card velocity engine.
* **Supplemental Draw Engines:**
  * **Bugenhagen, Wise Elder** (`{3}{G}`): Draws a card whenever a creature with power 4 or greater enters the battlefield, and distributes counters on ETB.
  * **Gallia, the Merrymaker** (`{R}{G}`): Provides haste to Satyrs and draws 2 cards (discarding 1 at random) whenever 3+ creatures attack.
  * **Bonders' Enclave**: Taps to draw a card whenever you control a creature with power 4 or greater.
* **Mana Acceleration:**
  * 1-drop and 2-drop dorks/ramp: **Birds of Paradise**, **Llanowar Elves**, **Nature's Lore**, **Rampant Growth**, **Bushwhack**, **Sol Ring**, **Wandertale Mentor**.
  * Explosive mana: **Nyxbloom Ancient** (`{4}{G}{G}{G}`) triples all mana production from permanents, turning landbases into lethal mana pools for massive activations.

---

### 5. Stompy Finishers & Direct-Damage Finishers
When combat stalls or opponents develop wide blockers, the deck transitions to deterministic closers:

* **Triumph of the Hordes** (`{2}{G}{G}`): Grants all creatures +1/+1, trample, and **infect**. A single swing from an 8-power Boo, amplified Wilson, or Halana & Alena eliminates opponents through poison counters.
* **Quartzwood Crasher** (`{2}{R}{G}{G}`, 6/6 Trample): Whenever one or more creatures deal combat damage to a player, creates an X/X Dinosaur Beast token where X is the total damage dealt. The resulting token can attack on subsequent turns or be flung for massive direct burn.
* **Old One Eye** (`{5}{G}`, 6/6): Grants universal trample to all creatures you control, enters with a 5/5 Beast token, and recurs from the graveyard by discarding cards.
* **Kessig Wolf Run**: Land-slot mana sink that grants trample and +X/+0 at instant speed. Synergizes with excess mana (or Nyxbloom Ancient) to turn any unblocked creature into a lethal strike or prep an enormous fling.
* **Exocrine** (`{X}{3}{R}`): Acts as a scalable sweeper dealing X damage to each other creature and player on ETB, leaving a massive body to fling postcombat.
* **Fling** (`{1}{R}`) & **Special Move** (`{1}{R}`): Backup instant-speed fling effects that convert creature power into face burn in response to removal or board wipes.

## Calculations & Worked Sequences

### The Command Zone Hamster Velocity Loop
Minsc & Boo creates a closed-loop engine cycle across two turns without requiring additional nonland cards from hand:

```
Turn 4: Cast Minsc & Boo ({2}{R}{G}, Loyalty: 3)
  └─> ETB creates Boo (1/1 Trample, Haste).
  └─> Activate +1: Target Boo -> Boo becomes a 4/4 Trample Haste (Loyalty: 4).
  └─> Combat: Attack with 4/4 Trample Haste (4 Combat Damage).

Turn 5: Upkeep: Boo is already on the battlefield.
  └─> Activate +1: Target Boo -> Boo becomes a 7/7 Trample Haste (Loyalty: 5).
  └─> Combat: Attack with 7/7 Trample Haste (7 Combat Damage).
  └─> Postcombat: Activate −2 (Loyalty: 3): Sacrifice Boo.
      └─> Deal 7 damage to ANY target (dome opponent or snipe key threat).
      └─> Draw 7 CARDS.

Turn 6: Upkeep: Minsc & Boo's trigger creates a fresh 1/1 Trample Haste Boo for {0} mana.
  └─> Loop repeats with a freshly replenished 7-card hand.
```

**Two-Turn Cycle Output:** **11 combat damage + 7 direct burn damage + 7 cards drawn**, powered exclusively by a 4-mana commander.

### Sazh Katzroy Exponential Counter Math
When **Sazh Katzroy** attacks alongside Boo:

1. **Turn 4:** Boo enters and receives Minsc's `+1` (3 counters $\rightarrow$ 4/4).
2. **Turn 5:** Sazh attacks. Sazh's trigger places 1 counter on Boo (4 counters), then doubles the counters on Boo (8 counters).
3. **Stats:** Boo is now a **9/9 Trample Haste**.
4. **Output:** Boo swings for 9 trample damage. In postcombat, Minsc sacrifices Boo to deal **9 damage to any target and draw 9 cards**.

---

### Raphael, the Muscle Damage Duplication
When **Raphael, the Muscle** is on the battlefield:
* Boo has three +1/+1 counters from Minsc's `+1` (4/4).
* Raphael doubles all damage dealt by creatures with counters.
* Combat: Boo attacks for **8 trample damage** ($4 \times 2$).
* Fling: Minsc's `−2` deals **8 direct burn damage** ($4 \times 2$) while drawing 4 cards.
* On Turn 5 with a 7/7 Boo: Combat deals **14 trample damage**, and the fling deals **14 direct damage** ($28\text{ total damage}$ from one creature).

---

### Death's Presence Perpetual Kinetic Battery
With **Death's Presence** (`{5}{G}`) on board:
* Sacrificing an 8/8 Boo to Minsc's `−2` deals 8 damage and draws 8 cards.
* Death's Presence triggers on Boo's death, placing **eight +1/+1 counters** onto **Wilson, Refined Grizzly** (turning Wilson into a 10/10 with vigilance, reach, trample, and ward `{2}`).
* On the following turn, Wilson attacks for 10 trample damage and can be sacrificed to *Fling* or Minsc's `−2` to pass 10+ counters onto the next creature, creating an unbroken damage chain.

## Inherent Weaknesses & Strategic Vulnerabilities

### 1. Planeswalker Target Fragility & Commander Reliance
Minsc & Boo serves as both the deck's primary card engine and its primary removal outlet.
* **Target Priority:** Opponents quickly recognize that Minsc & Boo generates an 8-card refill and 8-damage burn every two turns. Consequently, opposing boards prioritize attacking Minsc & Boo in combat or using targeted removal to eliminate the planeswalker before the `−2` activation.
* **Defense Gaps:** If Boo is attacking, Minsc is often left undefended against go-wide counter-attacks unless blocker bodies like *Wilson*, *Old One Eye*, or *Phantom Nantuko* are established.

### 2. Sorcery-Speed Timing Windows & Instant-Speed Interruption
* **Loyalty Timing:** Minsc & Boo's abilities can only be activated at sorcery speed on your turn.
* **The "Blown-Out on +1" Window:** If an opponent casts instant-speed spot removal (**Swords to Plowshares**, **Path to Exile**, **Rapid Hybridization**) on Boo in response to Minsc's `+1` activation, Boo is removed before combat. Minsc sits at high loyalty but has no creature to sacrifice that turn, delaying the card draw and burn cycle by a full turn.

### 3. Opponent-Dependent Threaten Reliability
* The deck carries 5 Threaten sorceries. Against creature-heavy midrange or voltron decks (**Syr Gwyn**, **Cloud**, **Doran**, **Sephiroth**), Threatens function as premium removal and combat pressure.
* Against creature-light spellslinger shells (**Y'shtola**, **Azula**) or artifact storm decks, Threaten spells can get trapped in hand with no legal or impactful targets, lowering hand efficiency.

### 4. Attack Taxes & Pillowfort Friction
* Because the velocity loop and token scaling depend on combat connections to trigger **Quartzwood Crasher**, **Sazh Katzroy**, and **Gallia**, pillowfort cards (**Ghostly Prison**, **Propaganda**, **Gluntch** stax) tax mana heavily.
* While the deck can pivot to direct-damage fling burn to bypass combat taxes, doing so cuts into the mana required to cast additional spells.

### 5. Lack of Stack-Based Protection
* In Gruul colors, the deck runs no counterspells. While **Heroic Intervention**, **Gaea's Gift**, and **Return the Favor** provide exceptional permanent and ability protection, mass exile wipes (**Farewell**) or non-damage sweepers that remove planeswalkers require the deck to rebuild from hand rather than defending the board on the stack.

## Piloting Protocol & Strategy

### Phase 1: Mana Acceleration & Board Setup (Turns 1 – 3)
* **Objective:** Accelerate mana curve to deploy Minsc & Boo a turn early and establish utility bodies.
* **Execution:**
  * Prioritize hands with 1- or 2-mana ramp (**Birds of Paradise**, **Llanowar Elves**, **Nature's Lore**, **Rampant Growth**, **Sol Ring**).
  * Deploy sticky or scaling companion creatures (**Wilson, Refined Grizzly**, **Casey Jones**, **Wandertale Mentor**).

### Phase 2: Deploying the Catapult & Cycling the Grip (Turns 4 – 5)
* **Objective:** Establish the Hamster velocity loop and reload the hand.
* **Execution:**
  * Cast **Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes**. Immediately activate `+1` targeting Boo and swing for 4 trample haste damage.
  * Turn 5: Activate `+1` to pump Boo to a 7/7, attack, and in the postcombat main phase evaluate table threats. If an opponent has a dangerous commander or planeswalker, activate `−2` to eliminate it and draw 7 fresh cards.
  * If holding protection (**Heroic Intervention**, **Return the Favor**), hold open mana to safeguard Minsc & Boo through the turn cycle.

### Phase 3: The Ballistic Close & Table Liquidation (Turn 6+)
* **Objective:** Eliminate remaining opponents through unblockable combat damage or direct-damage flings.
* **Execution:**
  * Use Threaten spells to steal opposing blockers and fling them to the face with Minsc or **Fling** / **Special Move**.
  * Deploy kinetic multipliers: swing with **Sazh** to double counters, drop **Raphael** to double all damage events, or resolve **Triumph of the Hordes** to deliver lethal infect trample damage.
  * Use **Kessig Wolf Run** (accelerated by **Nyxbloom Ancient**) to pump any trampling attacker into a lethal blow.
