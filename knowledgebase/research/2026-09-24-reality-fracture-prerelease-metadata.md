# Set Metadata Dossier: Reality Fracture (`fra`) & Reality Fracture Commander (`frc`)

- **Main Set Code:** `fra` (285 cards, expansion)
- **Commander Set Code:** `frc` (87 cards, commander)
- **Token Sets:** `tfra`, `tfrc`
- **Pre-Release Window:** **Friday, September 25, 2026 – Sunday, September 27, 2026**
- **Official Street Release:** **Friday, October 2, 2026**
- **Set Concept & Worldbuilding:** Jace Beleren constructs the **"Echoverse"**—an artificial multiverse engineered to supersede the scarred, imperfect prime reality. The set centers on high-concept planar divergences, spotlighting iconic characters walking radically inverted philosophical, moral, and mechanical paths.

## 1. Core Mechanics & New Keyword Actions

### 1. Prepare / Prepared (Layout: `prepare`)
A dual-face / integrated layout featuring a creature on the left/top and a linked spell on the right/bottom.
- **Rule / Trigger:** Creatures carry conditions under which they become *prepared* (e.g., *"At the beginning of each end step, if three or more creatures died this turn, this creature becomes prepared"*).
- **Execution:** While a creature is prepared, its controller may cast a copy of its integrated spell. Casting the spell **unprepares** the creature until the condition is met again.
- **EDH Assessment:** Built-in recursive spell-like card advantage that bypasses card disadvantage without occupying an extra deck slot.

### 2. Empower [Planeswalker] N (Primarily `Empower Jace`)
A keyword action driving the set's planeswalker token synergy.
- **Rule:** Put $ loyalty counters on a Jace token you control. If you don't control one, first create a blue Jace planeswalker token with:
  - `[-1]: Surveil 1`
  - `[-3]: Draw a card`
- **EDH Assessment:** Provides a reliable non-creature engine that builds passive card selection and card draw without investing full card slots into standalone planeswalker cards.

### 3. Behold [Name]
An additional casting cost or conditional threshold mechanic.
- **Rule:** *"To behold a [Name], choose a [Name] you control or reveal a [Name] card from your hand."* (e.g., *Countersculpt*: `{U}{U}` — As an additional cost to cast this spell, behold a Jace or pay `{1}`. Counter target spell. Empower Jace 1.)

### 4. Exhaust
A once-per-permanent restriction on high-impact activated abilities.
- **Rule:** *"Activate each exhaust ability only once."* (e.g., **Liliana the Repentant** `{1}{B}`: *Exhaust — `{5}{B}`: Return target creature or planeswalker card from your graveyard to the battlefield. Put a +1/+1 counter on Liliana. Activate only as a sorcery.*)
- **EDH Assessment:** Acts as a sorcery-speed "ultimate" on early-game bodies, avoiding repetitive loop abuse while providing built-in late-game mana sinks.

### 5. Returning Fan-Favorite Mechanics
- **Threshold** (7+ cards in graveyard)
- **Surveil** (Library sculpting / graveyard fuel)
- **Proliferate** (Interacting with planeswalkers, Jace tokens, and counters)
- **Convoke** & **Domain**
- **Landfall** & **Flashback**

## 2. Preconstructed Commander Deck: *Multiverse Reforged* (`frc`)

The Commander release for Reality Fracture consists of a single dedicated 4-color non-green preconstructed deck:

- **Deck Title:** *Multiverse Reforged*
- **Color Identity:** 4-Color Non-Green / Yore-Tiller (`{W}{U}{B}{R}`)
- **Core Archetype:** Token Production into Reality-Warping Polymorph / Big Threat Cheating
- **Face Commander:** **Jace, Multiverse Architect** (`{1}{W}{U}{B}{R}`, Loyalty: 4)
  - *Static:* At the beginning of combat on each opponent's turn, they may pay `{2}`. If they don't, creatures they control can't attack Jaces you control this turn.
  - `[+1]:` Draw two cards, then put a card from your hand on the bottom of your library.
  - `[-3]:` Exile another target planeswalker or creature you control. Reveal cards from the top of your library until you reveal a creature or planeswalker card. Put that card onto the battlefield and the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order.
  - *Commander:* Jace, Multiverse Architect can be your commander.
- **Alternate Commander:** **Nissa, Leyline Tamer** (`{3}{W}{U}{B}{R}`, 4/4 Elf Wizard)
  - Deathtouch, vigilance.
  - *Landfall* — Whenever a land you control enters, draw a card. Then if this is the first time this ability has resolved this turn, reveal cards from the top of your library until you reveal a creature card. Put that card onto the battlefield and the rest on the bottom of your library in a random order.

## 4. Limited Pre-Release & Draft Archetypes

The draft format is divided into 10 two-color archetypes: Allied pairs represent the **Colleges of Hexhaven** (led by the Elder Sphinx cycle), while Enemy pairs represent **Divergent Planeswalker Philosophies**:

### Allied Pairs: The Colleges of Hexhaven
1. **White-Blue (Fatehold):** Led by **Denzilore Fatehold** (`{1}{W}{U}{U}`). Surveil, card filtering, and tempo control.
2. **Blue-Black (Theorix):** Led by **Uldaros Theorix** (`{3}{U}{B}{B}`). Self-mill, graveyard value, and Threshold triggers.
3. **Black-Red (Stingerquill):** Led by **Ingris Stingerquill** (`{B}{R}{R}`). Aggro, direct burn, and sacrifice attrition.
4. **Red-Green (Konstrari):** Led by **Aerid Konstrari** (`{1}{R}{G}{G}`). Artifact synergies, power scaling, and ramp stompy.
5. **Green-White (Vigorbloom):** Led by **Kwia Vigorbloom** (`{3}{G}{W}{W}`). Lifegain triggers and +1/+1 counter engines.

### Enemy Pairs: Divergent Character Realities
1. **White-Black (Liliana's Attrition):** Aristocrats, morbid sacrifice, and lifedrain.
2. **Blue-Red (Saheeli's Artifice):** Artifact spellslinger and non-creature velocity.
3. **Black-Green (Vraska's Venom):** Deathtouch synergy, combat trades, and graveyard recursion.
4. **Red-White (Ajani's Vanguard):** Go-wide token aggro, combat buffs, and anthem effects.
5. **Green-Blue (Jace's Mastery):** Empower Jace loyalty engines, Landfall, and big-mana payoffs.

## 5. Strategic Evaluation for Commander Pod Play

1. **The Ur-Sphinx (Esper Sphinx Eminence):**
   - Immediately becomes the definitive Sphinx typal commander. The `{1}` mana reduction from the command zone curves out high-CMC sphinxes early, and the attack trigger turns combat into massive free spell velocity.
2. **Niv-Mizzet, Ghost Counsel (Orzhov Lifegain Draw):**
   - Synergizes with Soul Sisters, aristocrats, and lifelink. Allows converting burst lifegain into burst hand refills on a 1-to-1 ratio.
3. **Omnath, Locus of the Void (Colorless Landfall & Battery):**
   - Breakthrough commander for Colorless Eldrazi and Big Mana decks. Solves the colorless ramp bottleneck by storing unspent mana and turning every land into `{C}{C}`.
4. **Teferi's Reproach (Instant Complete Turn-Out):**
   - Unprecedented defensive and political piece in white. Can save an ally, defuse an archenemy's alpha-strike setup turn, or bypass an opponent's entire board for a full rotation.
5. **The Turbulent Land Cycle:**
   - Dual lands carrying basic land types (fetchable via typed fetches) that enter untapped throughout the early and mid-game, only entering tapped if opponents reach 8+ lands (punishing heavy ramp).
