# The Apex Rush — Salvage Pool & Donor Architecture

This document catalogs every card in the optimized **Winota, Joiner of Forces** build (*The Apex Rush*), detailing its source within Charles's (`fowlplays`) physical card collection ([`manabox-collection.csv`](../../podlist/charles/inventory/manabox-collection.csv)) and active deck archive in `knowledgebase/podlist/charles/decks/`.

---

## 1. Executive Summary: 100% Owned Card Parity

* **Total Cards in Deck:** 100 (1 Commander, 99 Mainboard)
* **Missing / Unowned Cards:** 0 ($0.00 cash outlay required)
* **Core Base:** *The Rush* (72 cards retained from the existing active physical deck)
* **Scavenged Upgrades:** 15 high-impact cards harvested across 6 active decks and the ManaBox bulk inventory
* **Mana Base Upgrades:** 13 Basics + 21 Nonbasics (including 3 untapped fetch/rainbow lands from other decks)

## 2. Donor Breakdown by Source

### A. Harvested from *The Gate of Babylon* / *The Armory* (WR Equipment)
* **Cloud, Midgar Mercenary:** 2-CMC Human Soldier Mercenary. When flipped by Winota or cast on curve, Cloud immediately searches the library for an Equipment card and puts it into your hand.
* **Zack Fair:** 1-CMC Human Soldier. Early aggressive curve filler; can be tutored by **Ranger-Captain of Eos** or cheated by Winota. Sacrifices for $\{1\}$ to give Winota indestructible at instant speed.
* **Freya Crescent:** 1-CMC Rat Knight. Crucial non-Human with flying on your turn, guaranteeing an evasive Winota trigger on Turn 3.
* **Conqueror's Flail:** Shuts off all opponent interaction during your turns. With Winota on board, opponents cannot cast spells, eliminating counterplay, spot removal, or flash blockers.
* **Umezawa's Jitte:** Easily tutored by Cloud. Provides repeatable creature removal, combat damage math manipulation, and life total stabilization.
* **Spire of Industry:** Untapped multi-color fixing land enabled by the deck's artifact acceleration and equipment.

### B. Harvested from *The Copied Factory* (UBR Wizards)
* **Gamble:** 1-CMC instant-speed tutor. Crucial consistency piece to assemble **Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker**, **Combat Celebrant**, or a protective **Silence** before a win attempt.
* **Prismatic Vista:** Premium untapped fetchland that fetches either basic Mountain or basic Plains untapped.
* **City of Brass:** Untapped 5-color land providing unconditional Boros fixing on Turn 1.

### C. Harvested from *The Best of Friends* (Mono-B)
* **Skullclamp:** Converts 1/1 Goblins (from Rabblemaster/Warboss), 1/1 Thopters (from Loyal Apprentice), or 0/1 Kobolds (from Kher Keep) into massive card draw for $\{1\}$.

### D. Harvested from *The Last Ride* (RG Vehicles)
* **Dolmen Gate:** "Prevent all combat damage that would be dealt to attacking creatures you control." One of the highest-impact secret tech cards in Winota: allows fragile 0/1s (Rograkh, Ornithopter) and 1/1 tokens to attack into any lethal blocker with zero penalty, ensuring infinite continuous Winota triggers.

### E. Harvested from *The Master Chef* (WG Counters)
* **Reprieve:** Unconditional white spell delay that bypasses "cannot be countered" clauses, returns opposing win conditions/sweepers to hand, and cantrips.

### F. Harvested from *The Lorehold Redux* (WR Graveyard)
* **Fabled Passage:** Untapped land fetch on turn 4+ to fix double-white and double-red requirements.

### G. Harvested from *The Queen of Theft* (UB Control) / Bulk
* **Mind Stone:** Untapped 2-CMC mana rock that cashes in for a card in the late game.

### H. Harvested from ManaBox Physical Bulk
* **Rionya, Fire Dancer:** 5-CMC Human Wizard. At the beginning of combat on your turn, creates hasty token copies of another creature. Combos with **Combat Celebrant** for an alternate infinite combat loop.

## 3. Cards Cut from Original *The Rush*

To accommodate the scavenged power cards, the following low-impact, narrow, or counter-synergistic cards from the original list were removed:

| Cut Card | Type | Rationale for Cut |
|---|---|---|
| **Blasphemous Act** | Sorcery | Counter-synergistic. Winota relies on a dense creature board; wiping your own board sets back your tempo irrevocably. |
| **Deflecting Palm** | Instant | Highly situational and reactive; does not advance Winota's proactive game plan. |
| **Disenchant** | Instant | Strict downgrade compared to **Cathar Commando**, **Skyclave Apparition**, and **Requisition Raid**. |
| **Erode** | Instant | Narrow artifact-only removal. |
| **Lorehold Charm** | Instant | 5-mana cost is too inefficient for a lean stax/aggro curve. |
| **Make a Stand** | Instant | 3 mana cost; outclassed by **Reprieve**, **Boros Charm**, and **Unbreakable Formation**. |
| **Impact Tremors** | Enchantment | Incidental burn is unnecessary when Winota attacks for 30–50+ combat damage. |
| **Endless Foot Assault** | Enchantment | Slow incremental engine; replaced by instant draw from **Skullclamp**. |
| **Kinbinding** | Enchantment | High CMC aura with low floor. |
| **Avalanche of Sector 7** | Creature | Low-impact trigger; replaced by direct tutor **Cloud, Midgar Mercenary**. |
| **Kinscaer Sentry** | Creature | Modest vanilla stats; replaced by game-ending combo piece **Rionya, Fire Dancer**. |
| **Erkenbrand, Lord of Westfold** | Creature | Overcosted anthem effect; replaced by the 1-CMC tutor target **Zack Fair**. |
| **Labyrinth of Skophos** | Land | Expensive 4-mana colorless activation; cut for untapped color fixing (**Prismatic Vista**). |
| **Sunscorched Divide** | Land | Slow filter land; cut for **City of Brass**. |
