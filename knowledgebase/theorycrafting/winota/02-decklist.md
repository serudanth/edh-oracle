## Notes

- 100% card parity with fowlplays's owned physical inventory and active deck archive. Zero missing cards.
- Entire equipment package, dead-clause cards (Zack Fair, Freya Crescent, Swiftblade Vindicator), artifact-conditional lands (Spire of Industry), and low-yield temporary token generators (Harried Dronesmith) removed.
- Refined creature density at **51 total creatures** (27 Humans including Witch Enchanter MDFC, 24 Non-Humans).
- Upgraded with unconditional stax deterrent **Michiko Konda, Truth Seeker**, permanent swarm engines & finishers **Silverwing Squadron** and **Sami, Ship's Engineer**, flying/vigilance enabler **Momo, Playful Pet**, life engine / tutor target **Soul Warden**, and untapped dual **Sundown Pass**.
- Single-trigger whiff rate on 6 cards sits at **12.90%** (87.1% hit rate); two-trigger whiff rate drops to **1.66%**, making multi-attacker triggers virtually guaranteed hits.
- Deterministic combo: Kiki-Jiki, Mirror Breaker + Combat Celebrant (infinite combats).
- Non-creature package trimmed to 16 absolute essentials: fast mana rocks, Rule of Law locks (Deafening Silence, High Noon), combat safety (Dolmen Gate, Reconnaissance), free/unconditional protection (Clever Concealment, Boros Charm, Silence, Reprieve), and premier interaction (Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Gamble).
