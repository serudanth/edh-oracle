## Commander Snapshot

* **Commander:** [Maralen, Fae Ascendant](https://scryfall.com/card/ecl/233/maralen-fae-ascendant) ({2}{B}{G}{U})
* **Type:** Legendary Creature — Elf Faerie Noble (3/4, Flying)
* **Color Identity:** Sultai ({B}{G}{U})
* **Core Mechanics:** 
  1. *Whenever Maralen or another Elf or Faerie you control enters, exile the top two cards of target opponent's library.*
  2. *Once each turn, you may cast a spell with mana value less than or equal to the number of Elves and Faeries you control from among cards exiled with Maralen this turn without paying its mana cost.*
