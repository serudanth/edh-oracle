## 2. Donor Deck A: The Dirt Kicker (Tifa Lockhart — Mono-G)

### Landfall & Extra Land Drops
* **`Azusa, Lost but Seeking`** — Top-tier engine piece: gives +2 additional land drops per turn.
* **`Erinis, Gloom Stalker`** — Deathtouch attacker that brings back a land from the graveyard to the battlefield tapped on every attack.
* **`Harrow`** — Sacrifices a land as an additional cost (which hits the yard and gains Dredge 2) to put two lands onto the battlefield untapped.
* **`Traverse the Outlands`** — Mass basic land ramp scaling off Necrobloom's 7 toughness (fetches up to 7 basics at once!).
* **`Elemental Teachings`** — 5-MV instant (*Realms Uncharted* effect): searches 4 differently-named lands. An opponent puts 2 in the graveyard (they gain Dredge 2 and trigger Gitrog!) and 2 onto the battlefield tapped (triggering Necrobloom twice!).
* **`Summon: Titan`** — Phenomenal 3-in-1 piece: Chapter I mills 5 cards; Chapter II is a built-in **`Splendid Reclamation`** returning *all* lands from your graveyard to the battlefield tapped; Chapter III gives massive $+X/+X$ trample pump based on lands controlled.
* **`Traveling Chocobo`** — 3-MV powerhouse: plays lands from top of library, and **doubles all landfall triggers** from lands and Birds (doubles Necrobloom, Evolution Sage, Tireless Provisioner, Racetrack!).
* **`Chocobo Racetrack`** — Landfall artifact engine: creates 2/2 Birds whenever a land enters that pump with future land drops. Doubled by Traveling Chocobo into two Birds per land drop!

### Utility, Card Advantage & Protection
* **`Abundance`** — Replaces draws with targeted land/nonland reveals. Prevents self-mill death when dredging deeply, and ensures you never whiff on land drops.
* **`Spore Frog`** — Repeatable Fog lock when combined with creature recursion.
* **`Haywire Mite`** — Cheap noncreature exile that gains 2 life; easily reusable.
* **`Rishkar's Expertise`** — Draws 7 cards off Necrobloom's 7 toughness and casts a 5-MV spell for free.
* **`Gaea's Gift`** & **`Snakeskin Veil`** — Cheap instant-speed protection for Necrobloom.
* **`Birds of Paradise`** (Foil) — S-tier 1-drop mana fixer.
* **`Sol Ring`** & **`Arcane Signet`** — Format staples.

### Lands
* **`Demolition Field`** — Strip Mine effect that tutors a basic, puts itself in the graveyard, and can be dredged back every turn.
* **`Evolving Wilds`** & **`Terramorphic Expanse`** — Budget fetchlands that loop with Dredge 2 to guarantee 2 landfall triggers per turn.
* Basic **`Forest`** stock.

## 3. Donor Deck B: The Master Chef (Lae'zel // Master Chef — Selesnya WG)

### Land & Creature Synergy
* **`Sakura-Tribe Elder`** (Foil) — Self-sacrificing land ramp that triggers landfall and can be recurred.
* **`Fertilid`** — Activated land tutor that converts mana/counters into lands onto the battlefield.
* **`Evolution Witness`** (Foil) — Adapt/counter trigger that returns any permanent from the graveyard to hand.
* **`Evolution Sage`** — Proliferates on every landfall trigger.

### Premium Interaction & Wipes
* **`Skyclave Apparition`** — Premium exile removal on a creature body.
* **`Stroke of Midnight`** — Unconditional nonland permanent destruction.
* **`Reprieve`** — White pseudo-counterspell / tempo play that draws a card (triggering Dredge).
* **`Farewell`** & **`Austere Command`** — Modal board wipes. (Note: *Farewell* exiles graveyards, so use with caution when your own bin is loaded).

### Ramp & Velocity
* **`Nature's Lore`** — Forest tutor (grabs typed duals/triomes untapped).
* **`Cultivate`** & **`Explore`** — Classic ramp and velocity.

### Lands & Mana Base (Selesnya Duals + Utility)
* **`Ash Barrens`** — Basic landcycling for $\{1\}$ hits the graveyard immediately and can be Dredged back off the cycle draw.
* **`Scattered Groves`** — Plains Forest dual with Cycling $\{2\}$ (functions as an engine piece with Dredge 2).
* **`Selesnya Sanctuary`** — Bounce land: guarantees land drops by returning a land to hand.
* **`Canopy Vista`** — Fetchable dual with basic land types.
* **`Brushland`**, **`Sunpetal Grove`**, **`Fortified Village`**, **`Temple of Plenty`**, **`Sungrass Prairie`** — Diverse named Selesnya duals (helps hit the 7-unique-name threshold).
* **`Stirring Wildwood`** — Manland for diversity.
* **`Nesting Grounds`** — Utility land.
* **`Command Tower`** & **`Path of Ancestry`** — Multi-color staples.

## 4. Salvage Summary

* **Immediate High-Synergy Inclusions:** ~30–35 cards ready to slot straight into Necrobloom.
* **Missing Elements Needed to Complete Deck:**
  1. **Black Mana Base:** Golgari/Orzhov duals, fetchlands, and black cycling/utility lands (`Bojuka Bog`, `Barren Moor`, `Golgari Rot Farm`, `Overgrown Tomb`).
  2. **Free Discard Outlets:** (`Skirge Familiar`, `Putrid Imp`, `Zombie Infestation`) to unlock the Gitrog/Necrobloom dredge engine.
  3. **Mass Land Resurgence:** (`Splendid Reclamation`, `Aftermath Analyst`, `Lumra, Bellow of the Woods`).
  4. **The Skullclamp / Aristocrats Engine:** (`Skullclamp`, `Corpse Knight`, `Warren Soultrader`, `Mirkwood Bats`).
