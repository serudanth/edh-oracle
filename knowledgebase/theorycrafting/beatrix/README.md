## Core Strategy Overview

* **Commander:** [Beatrix, Loyal General](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/12/beatrix-loyal-general) ({4}{W}{W})
* **Identity:** Mono-White ({W})
* **The Concept:** While the rest of the pod runs aggressive Boros/Mardu/Naya extra combats, Beatrix establishes a defensive, tactical armory. At the start of combat, she snaps **every piece of equipment on the board** onto a single creature for {0} mana, completely breaking high-equip artillery ([Ultima Weapon](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/159/ultima-weapon), [Colossus Hammer](https://scryfall.com/card/afc/202/colossus-hammer), [Aettir and Priwen](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/142/aettir-and-priwen)).
* **Lieutenant Synergy:** [Adelbert Steiner](https://scryfall.com/card/fic/1/adelbert-steiner) provides an early lifelinking body that scales with equipment count, turning weapon attachments into 20–40+ life swings.
* **Salvage & Collection:** Reclaims the white and colorless core of *The Gate of Babylon* (Bruenor), integrates FF set pulls (Beatrix, Steiner, Elena), and taps into utility gems from *Secrets of Strixhaven* ([Bitterthorn](https://scryfall.com/card/moc/45/bitterthorn-nissas-animus), [War Room](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/53/war-room), [Tocasia's Welcome](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/29/tocasias-welcome), [Akroma's Will](https://scryfall.com/card/soa/1/akromas-will)).
