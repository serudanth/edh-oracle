## Notes

- Re-tuned to replace $650+ in generic cEDH staples with realistic, high-synergy budget powerhouses.
- Clean 2x2 Modular Infinite Combo Package:
  - Token Spawners: Scurry Oak ($0.50) & Herd Baloth ($0.38)
  - Counter Placers: Rosie Cotton of South Lane ($0.35) & Ivy Lane Denizen ($0.25)
  - Any cross-combination creates infinite tokens and an infinitely large creature!
- Shared Summons (5-mana instant): Cast on opponent's end step to tutor ANY winning pair directly to your hand.
- Zero rules friction: Replaced the clunky Devoted Druid / Swift Reconfiguration interaction with 100% on-theme +1/+1 counters and tokens.
- Sourced from collection: Re-uses 80 cards already owned across The Dirt Kicker, The Master Chef, and The Hugs stub.
- Out-of-pocket upgrade cost: ~$20–$25 across all non-owned singles.
