## Strategic Blueprint & Core Pillars

* **Commanders:** **Tymna the Weaver** ({1}{W}{B}) & **Kraum, Ludevic's Opus** ({3}{U}{R})
* **Color Identity:** Sans-Green / WUBR ({W}{U}{B}{R})
* **Physical Anchors in Hand:** Charles physically owns the Final Fantasy prints of both commanders (**Tymna the Weaver**, **Kraum, Ludevic's Opus**), as well as the primary win condition: **Thassa's Oracle** and **Tainted Pact**.
* **The "Zero-Dollar" Architecture:** Rather than buying high-end fast mana, the deck leverages Charles's active decks (**The Copied Factory**, **The Rush**, **The Queen of Theft**, **The Crystal Braves**) to proxy format titans like *Rhystic Study*, *Demonic Tutor*, *Silence*, *Ranger-Captain of Eos*, *Boromir*, *Drannith Magistrate*, and *Pact of Negation*.
* **The Uncounterable Tutor Engine:** Uses *Step Through* (Wizardcycling {2}) to tutor *Thassa's Oracle* (a Merfolk Wizard) directly to hand at instant speed without casting a spell, bypassing stack interaction.
* **Strict Singleton Mana Base:** Carefully balances 33 untapped and fetchable lands with zero duplicate names to ensure *Tainted Pact* operates flawlessly.
