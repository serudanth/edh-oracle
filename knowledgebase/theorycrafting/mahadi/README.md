## The Core 5 Engine

The deck was designed around an initial theorycrafting prompt incorporating five specific high-friction cards:

1. **War's Toll** ({3}{R}): Forces opponents to tap out all lands when tapping one, and forces all creatures an opponent controls to attack if any attack.
2. **Sulfuric Vortex** ({1}{R}{R}): Shuts down all lifegain across the table and burns each player for 2 damage on their upkeep.
3. **Ancient Runes** ({2}{R}): Burns each player on their upkeep for damage equal to the number of artifacts they control.
4. **Words of War** ({2}{R}): Replaces card draws with 2 damage to any target for {1}.
5. **Solphim, Mayhem Dominus** ({2}{R}{R}): Doubles all noncombat damage dealt to opponents and their permanents, breaking parity on Sulfuric Vortex, Ancient Runes, Syr Konrad, and Words of War.
